## Answer

Nebius Group N.V. is a technology company headquartered in Amsterdam, the Netherlands, and listed on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol NBIS. It provides AI-centric cloud platforms and operates through brands like TripleTen, an edtech platform for re-skilling in technology careers, and Avride, which develops autonomous driving technology. The company was formerly known as Yandex N.V. and rebranded in August 2024. The sources do not provide specific details on the valuation or revenue of the company beyond mentioning a range of $50M to $800M and revenue of around $41M in 2025.
