# What fine-tuning / post-training and dedicated endpoints does Nebius Token Factory offer?


Query: `Nebius Token Factory fine-tuning post-training dedicated endpoints` (Tavily advanced search, fetched 2026-10-30T14:27:03.134Z)



## Answer

The sources indicate that Nebius Token Factory offers fine-tuning post-training capabilities for various models, including DeepSeek AI v3, GPT-OSS 20B/120B, and Qwen3 Coder 480B, with deployment via dedicated endpoints that provide autoscaling, zero-retention, and predictable latency. The platform supports both long-context supervised fine-tuning on full model weights or LoRA adapters and distillation to cut inference costs and latency. However, training prices and specific data limits are not stated in the sources. The deployment options are exclusively via dedicated endpoints, and the sources do not provide a definitive answer on the training prices.
