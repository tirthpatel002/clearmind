# How is the Nebius x NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon judged?


Query: `Nebius NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon judging criteria` (Tavily basic search, fetched 2026-10-30T14:28:06.504Z)



## Answer

The Nebius x NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon judging criteria include equally weighted evaluation based on the completeness, viability, and potential impact of the feedback provided on the Nebius tools and NVIDIA models used. Submissions must run on either Nebius Token Factory or Nebius AI Cloud and use at least one NVIDIA open source model. The judging period is from December 1 to December 15, 2026, and winners will be announced around January 11, 2027. The hackathon submission period ends on October 30, 2026.
