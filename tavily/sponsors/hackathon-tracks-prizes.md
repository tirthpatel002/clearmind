# What are the Nebius x NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon tracks and prizes?


Query: `Nebius NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon tracks prizes` (Tavily basic search, fetched 2026-10-30T14:28:03.122Z)



## Answer

The Nebius x NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon offers a prize pool of over $50,000, including a grand prize of $20,000, second place at $10,000, and third place at $6,000. Track winners receive an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano, and the best use of Tavily is awarded $3,000. Additionally, there are city winner awards of $500 each for 20 winners and a most valuable feedback award of $100 plus an NVIDIA swag pack for 10 winners. Each project can receive one overall award or one track award plus one bonus award, but not multiple overall prizes.
