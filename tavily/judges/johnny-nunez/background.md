Johnny Núñez is an AI Developer Advocate at NVIDIA, focusing on Physical AI and Robotics. He works at the intersection of simulation, foundation models, teleoperation, and edge computing, utilizing tools like Isaac Sim, GR00T, Jetson Thor, and DGX Spark. He is co-organizing the "The Full Stack of Physical AI" tutorial at CVPR 2026 in Denver, which covers simulation, foundation models, and edge deployment for next-generation robotics. Núñez is also pursuing a PhD in Computer Vision at the University of Barcelona, researching Human-Robot-Object Interactions (HROIs) at the intersection of generative AI, robotics, psychology, and neuroscience.
