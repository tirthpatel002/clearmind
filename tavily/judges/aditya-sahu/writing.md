## Answer

Aditya Sahu is a technical marketing engineer on the NVIDIA Jetson Edge AI Platform team. He is responsible for driving the adoption and showcasing the technical capabilities of the platform. He actively engages with the NVIDIA developer community, advocating for AI model inference and SDKs on Jetson. He previously worked at Synaptics, focusing on building Edge AI solutions, and held ML and software engineering roles at Nuvoton and Netcracker. He holds an MS in Artificial Intelligence from San José State University and a BS in Computer Science from Siddaganga Institute of Technology. His recent work includes a blog post titled "Frontier Reasoning Reaches the Edge: How to Deploy and Optimize Models on NVIDIA Jetson," published on September 4, 2026.
